Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a Graphic Equalizer?
A graphic equalizer allows you to boost or attenuate specific frequency bands across the audio spectrum using vertical sliders.
How does Parametric EQ differ?
Parametric equalizers offer complete control over center frequencies, gain level, and Q-factor bandwidth for surgical sound shaping.
Is this online equalizer tool free?
Yes, AWDEV EQ Generator is 100% free and open-source for all music creators, developers, and audio enthusiasts.
