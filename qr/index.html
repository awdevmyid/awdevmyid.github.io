Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this QR code generator completely free?
Yes, AWDEV QR Generator is 100% free and requires no registration, subscription, or login.
How do I generate a QR code for a file?
Click on the 'Upload File' tab, select any text, image, or document file from your device, and the tool will automatically convert it into a scannable data URI QR code.
Can I download the generated QR code?
Yes! Click the 'Download QR Image' button below the preview container to save your custom QR code directly to your device.
