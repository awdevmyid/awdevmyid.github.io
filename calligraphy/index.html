Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I use the virtual Arabic keyboard?
Simply click on any Arabic character button on the virtual keyboard grid, and it will be appended instantly to your active text input box.
Can I download my calligraphy as PNG or PDF?
Yes! Click the 'Download PNG' or 'Download PDF' buttons to export your high-resolution canvas artwork directly to your device.
Is this Arabic calligraphy generator free?
Yes, all tools on AWDEV are 100% free and open for public use without any registration or login required.
